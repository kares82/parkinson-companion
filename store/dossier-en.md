# Parkinson Companion — one-page brief

*A free tracking diary for the family carers of a person living with Parkinson's disease.*

## The problem
Family carers — often a spouse aged 65–80 — handle episodes, medication and
incidents at home, alone. At the neurology appointment everything rests on
memory. Existing apps are complicated, English-only, full of accounts and ads,
and need a connection.

## The solution
A **very simple** app, designed for a shaking hand and an older user: one large
button to start an episode, one-tap call for help, and a diary of medication,
wellbeing and incidents (falls, freezing). It produces a **clear report for the
neurologist**: episodes by time of day against dose times, frequency and
duration before/after a treatment change, adherence, falls and freezing.

## What makes it different
- **Free** for families, and ad-free.
- **Fully offline**: works with no connection, opens instantly.
- **Collects no data**: everything stays on the phone. Nothing is sent to the
  internet. No account, no tracking.
- **French and English**, large type, high contrast, dark mode at night.
- **User-owned backup** (a file) — the person stays in control.

## What it is not
A tracking diary. It is not a medical device, makes no diagnosis, gives no
medical advice, and does not replace a healthcare professional or the emergency
services.

## Who it helps
Family carers, the people living with the illness, and neurologists who finally
get data instead of recollection. Also useful to care homes, home-care
agencies and patient associations.
